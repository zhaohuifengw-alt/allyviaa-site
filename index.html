Why patients are choosing China

China has quietly become one of the world's most capable healthcare systems — and for international patients, one of the best-value. Four reasons it works:

Honest prices

The same implant brands, scanners and surgical techniques — at 20–50% of what you'd pay at home, because of lower operating costs, not lower standards.

No waiting lists

Skip the 6–18 month queue for scans, dental work or surgery. Most Allyviaa patients start treatment within 1–3 weeks of their plan.

Serious expertise

China's top hospitals handle patient volumes few Western centres can match — and experience shows in outcomes. Shanghai's hospitals alone serve patients from 100+ countries.

Treatments you can't get yet

China now originates a third of the world's drugs in development. Some innovative therapies reach patients here first — one more reason specialists refer cases to China.

Questions, answered straight

Is medical treatment in China safe?+

We only coordinate care at China's top-tier public hospitals (Grade 3A, the highest national rating) and established international departments that treat foreign patients daily — China's leading hospitals handled around 1.28 million international patients in 2025. You see the hospital's credentials and your team's profile before committing to anything.

How much can I save compared with treatment at home?+

Most patients save 50–80% versus US, UK or Australian private prices — for example a dental implant costing US$5,000–6,000 in the US typically costs US$700–1,200 in China, using the same international implant brands. Your quote is itemised before you travel; there are no surprise add-ons.

Do the doctors and staff speak English?+

International departments in Shanghai and Beijing work in English daily. Your Allyviaa coordinator also accompanies you to appointments and translates whenever needed — and your medical reports are provided in English.

How long does the whole process take?+

The free case review usually takes 1–2 business days. Most patients start treatment within 1–3 weeks of deciding. A dental or check-up trip typically takes 3–7 days on the ground; surgical cases are planned individually.

What visa do I need?+

Many nationalities can use visa-free entry or the 240-hour transit policy; others apply for a visitor or medical (S) visa. Once your appointment is confirmed, the hospital can issue an invitation letter to support your application, and we guide you through the paperwork.

What about follow-up once I'm home?+

You leave with complete English medical records, imaging and a treatment summary your home doctor can act on. Your coordinator remains available by email, and remote reviews with your treating team in China can be arranged.
